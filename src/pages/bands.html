Our Bands

Ten roaming bands, one signature: live music that moves with your guests.

A roaming band is a fully acoustic, mobile group. There is no stage, no cables and no barrier between the musicians and the room. The band walks, plays and sings among your guests, so every corner of the venue becomes the front row. It is the signature of The Roaming Agency, and the reason our events feel so personal.

Based on the French Riviera and performing worldwide, our ten bands cover everything from vocal harmony and French chanson to rock'n'roll and jazz, in up to five languages. Each one has its own character, repertoire and story. Browse the lineup below and click any band to see videos, reviews and details, or send us a note and we will match the right band to your wedding, corporate event or private party.
