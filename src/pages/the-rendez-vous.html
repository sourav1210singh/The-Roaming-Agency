More About The Rendez-Vous

A roaming band, equally known as a strolling band, is an unplugged and therefore mobile group. The musicians are free to mingle amongst the spectators, making use of all the space to offer a personalised and interactive performance. They generally sing without microphones.

The bands generally perform 90 to 120 minutes of live music, which can be split into several sets across the key moments of the event, within a 6 hours presence on site.

The band's repertoire covers many eras, languages and styles. Because every crowd is different, the musicians watch, listen and adjust the set in real time. You can highlight the songs you would like to hear from the setlist. Once the party begins, the best results come from letting the band improvise.

A strolling band has no strong amplification. The concept involves bringing the music directly to the guests, passing between and around the groups or the tables. It is a close proximity performance, loud enough to get over 160 guests dancing. It cannot be compared with the kind of volume produced by a DJ through his speakers, but that is not the objective. For larger events, it is possible to have the band plug into a sound system with wireless mics, or even have them on stage with an amplified setup: guitar and bass amps, traditional acoustic drumkit and so on.
