The Rendez-Vous

The Rendez-Vous take you back to the future, where every era's greatest hits are reborn for the present moment.
Timeless classics, reimagined: the songs you grew up with, dressed in a sound that feels brand new.
A repertoire that travels through the decades — from vintage swing and soul to today's chart-toppers — woven together in five languages.
An international cast of multilingual musicians, trained in the finest conservatoires and at home on stages from the French Riviera to the far side of the world.
Nostalgic yet fresh, retro yet modern: a meeting point between the music you love and the moment you're living.

Beyond the songs themselves, The Rendez-Vous stand out for the warmth and chemistry they bring on stage — a feeling you sense from the very first note.
Their elegant, retro-meets-modern aesthetic finds its place naturally at the heart of exceptional events worldwide.

More About The Rendez-Vous

A roaming band, also known as a strolling band, is an unplugged and therefore mobile group. The musicians perform while mingling with your guests throughout the venue without microphones, offering an intimate, interactive experience.

The Rendez-Vous specialise in taking timeless classics and reimagining them for today — back to the future. We blend the nostalgia of decades past with a fresh, modern sound, so every generation in the room hears something they love.

Our repertoire travels through the decades — vintage swing and soul, pop and rock anthems, French classics and today's chart-toppers — performed in five languages. We adapt the set to each unique audience, and specific titles can always be requested in advance.

We are based in Nice, on the French Riviera, and travel anywhere our music is needed — from Monaco and Saint-Tropez to Lake Como, Dubai and beyond.

The standard offering is 3 sets of 30 minutes each, spread across the key moments of your event. Larger or premium events can book extended options: 4 sets of 30 minutes, or 3 sets of 40 minutes.
