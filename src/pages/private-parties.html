More About The Brotherockers

A roaming band, also known as a strolling band, is an unplugged and therefore mobile group. The Brotherockers perform while mingling with your guests throughout the venue without microphones, bringing the music directly to you for an intimate, interactive experience.

The standard offering is 3 sets of 30 minutes each, spread across the key moments of your event. Larger or premium events can book extended options: 4 sets of 30 minutes, or 3 sets of 40 minutes.

The Brotherockers' repertoire spans pop, rock, soul, jazz and Latin, performed in five languages and across all styles. As the flagship roaming band, we tailor the setlist to each unique audience, and specific titles can always be requested in advance.

We are based in Nice, on the French Riviera, and travel worldwide wherever our music is needed — from Monaco and Saint-Tropez to Lake Como, London, Dubai and beyond.

A strolling band uses no heavy amplification — the concept is to bring the music directly to your guests. The Brotherockers' acoustic setup is loud enough to get over 150 people dancing, without DJ-level volume.
