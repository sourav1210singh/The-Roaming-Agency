The Kingsmen

The Kingsmen are a five-piece vocal powerhouse, a quintet built around the very best singers in the industry.
From the first note, five voices lock into tight, regal harmonies that fill the room and command every eye in it.
Each member is a soloist in his own right, trained in the finest schools and seasoned on the world's most demanding stages.
Together they move as one, weaving lead lines and lush backing into a sound that feels both effortless and majestic.

Their commanding stage presence turns every performance into a true spectacle, drawing guests in and keeping them captivated from the very first note.
International and multilingual, based on the French Riviera and performing worldwide, The Kingsmen carry a vast repertoire across five languages to unite every generation and every guest.
Refined, charismatic and impeccably dressed, they bring a touch of royalty to the most exceptional events.

More About The Kingsmen

The Kingsmen are a five-piece vocal powerhouse — a quintet built around five of the best singers in the industry. Each is a soloist in his own right, and together they create tight, regal harmonies with a commanding stage presence that sets them apart.

The standard offering is 3 sets of 30 minutes each, spread across the key moments of your event. Larger or premium events can book extended options: 4 sets of 30 minutes, or 3 sets of 40 minutes.

The Kingsmen's repertoire spans a wide variety of songs in five languages and across all styles, all carried by five-part vocal harmonies. We adapt the performance to each unique audience, and specific titles can always be requested in advance.

We are based in Nice, on the French Riviera, and travel anywhere our music is needed — from Monaco and Saint-Tropez to Lake Como, Dubai and beyond.

As a vocal-led quintet, The Kingsmen shine in rich five-part harmony — from intimate a cappella moments to fuller arrangements with light accompaniment. We tailor the setup to the room and the moment, keeping the voices always at the heart of the performance.
